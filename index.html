Bookmark this, or add it to your home screen. It opens this page on any device, already connected, with no key or sign-in needed. Keep it private: like the lead links, it gives access to the sheets.

3. Send each lead their link

These are the spreadsheets in the app's folder. Each link opens that sheet only, with no login. Treat a link like a key.

4. Add a concert or event

Goes into every sheet in date order. If that date is already in a sheet (e.g. a Wednesday rehearsal that becomes a concert), its type is changed instead of adding a new column.

5. Attendance summary

Attendance = singers marked ✓ as a share of everyone with a mark for that date (blank cells don’t count). Tap or hover a chart for figures.
